## 2026-2027 Auditions

### Rice Acapella Info Session
**Date:** September 3rd @ 7 PM, Herring 100  
Come meet all 6 Rice acapella groups! You’ll learn about all the different groups and can ask any questions you have.

### Phils Audition Dates
*Please only attend one.*
- **Day 1:** September 9th @ 6 PM, Jones PDR
- **Day 2:** September 11th @ 7 PM, Jones PDR

### How It Works
* **No Prep Needed:** You don’t need to prepare a song or have anything ready! Just bring yourself
* **Workshop:** We start with a group workshop to teach you everything you need for the audition.
* **10-Minute Slots:** After the workshop, you'll sign up for a short individual slot later that day.

### Callbacks
Once all auditions are over, we will notify you via email regarding a second round of callbacks!
